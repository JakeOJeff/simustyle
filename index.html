Everything in one editor

From empty scene to playable demo.

The tools you'd expect from a desktop 3D package, tuned to get an idea moving in minutes.

Scene editor

Viewport, hierarchy, and inspector. Orbit, pan, parent objects, and right-click to drop shapes, lights, and cameras where you point.

Real physics

Rigid bodies and collisions powered by Rapier. Press Play to simulate, Stop to snap back to your edit.

Plain-English scripts

Write behaviors like when clicked spin fast. Events, variables, keys, and messages — no boilerplate.

Keyframe animation

A timeline with 30+ easing curves, a bezier editor, and one-click presets like bounce, float, and pulse.

Export anywhere

Ship your scene as a GLB model, a standalone HTML page anyone can open, or a WebM video.

AI assistant

Optional: describe a scene in words and let the assistant build or edit it for you. Works with any OpenAI-compatible model.

Scripting

Logic you can read out loud.

SimuStyle's script language reads like instructions to a friend. Respond to clicks, keys, and collisions, keep score, broadcast messages, and animate over time — then press Play.

  • Events: game start, forever, clicks, keys, touches
  • Tweens: move up 1 over 0.25 seconds
  • Tunable settings exposed as Inspector sliders
